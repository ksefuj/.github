# Contributing to ksefuj

Thanks for helping. Each repository has its own setup notes in its README; the monorepo has a
detailed guide in
[ksefuj/CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/ksefuj/ksefuj/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md). These rules
apply everywhere:

- **Open an issue first** for anything bigger than a small fix, so we can agree on the approach.
- **Cite the source** for any KSeF, FA(3) or tax rule: the Ministry of Finance information sheet,
  the XSD, the KSeF manual or the act. We don't merge rules that can't be traced to an official
  document.
- **Never post real invoices.** Replace names, NIPs, addresses and bank accounts with made-up data
  before attaching XML to an issue or PR.
- **Conventional commits**: `feat:`, `fix:`, `docs:`, `refactor:`, `test:`, `chore:` and so on.
- **English** for code, comments, commits and issues. FA(3) element names (`Podmiot1`, `FaWiersz`)
  stay as in the schema. Issues in Polish are welcome too.

By contributing you agree that your work is licensed under Apache 2.0 and that you follow the
[Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
